How the math works

Each expense is divided into a responsibility share for each parent — evenly by default, or by a custom percentage, a fixed dollar amount, or 100% to one parent. Whoever pays covers the other parent's share, so the other parent owes them that amount. Disputed expenses are excluded from the balance until approved. Settlement payments reduce the running balance. The monthly statement carries any prior balance forward, adds the month's activity, and shows who owes whom at month end.
