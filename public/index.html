How the math works

Every expense is split evenly. Whoever pays covers the other parent's half, so the other parent owes them 50% of that expense. Settlement payments reduce the running balance. The monthly statement carries any prior balance forward, adds the month's expenses and payments, and shows who owes whom at month end.
